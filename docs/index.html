Shopping Spreadsheet Quality Checklist

Review required fields, duplicate URLs, record status, and stale check dates before a community shopping spreadsheet is shared publicly.

Open the toolkit on GitHub

What the toolkit checks

Structure

Confirm the expected columns exist and required values are not blank.

URLs

Flag malformed HTTP or HTTPS destinations and duplicate normalized links.

Status

Keep review states limited to a small documented vocabulary.

Freshness

Warn when a record has not received a real manual check within the chosen interval.

A simple review workflow

  1. Export the spreadsheet with the documented column names.
  2. Run the local audit and fix structural errors.
  3. Open destinations manually and confirm their final domain and page purpose.
  4. Update status and review dates only after the checks are complete.

Important limits

The tool does not verify authenticity, seller reliability, price, stock, QC photos, warehouse handling, or shipping outcomes. Those details can change and must be checked on the destination platform.

Related public example
