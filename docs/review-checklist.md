# Spreadsheet review checklist

Use this checklist after the automated audit.

## Data structure

- Required columns are present and use consistent names.
- Duplicate URLs have been removed or intentionally documented.
- Categories follow one taxonomy.
- Status values reflect the latest manual review.

## Destination checks

- The final destination is the expected domain and page.
- The link does not end at a login wall, shortener, thin redirect, or unrelated page.
- Product name, images, price, stock, size, and QC information are treated as changeable data.
- Broken or changed destinations are marked for review instead of silently retained.

## Publishing notes

- The public description explains what the spreadsheet does and does not verify.
- Update dates correspond to actual changes.
- Commercial or referral links are clearly contextualized.
- No official, verified, guaranteed, exclusive, ranking, or user-review claim is added without evidence.
